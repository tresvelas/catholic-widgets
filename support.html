Support

Questions, corrections to a reading, or a problem with the app? Write to treslevas@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Add a widget to the Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Catholic Widgets, choose a size and tap Add Widget.

Add a widget to the Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and pick Catholic Widgets.

The widget shows yesterday's Gospel

Open the app once so it can load the latest readings. iOS decides when widgets refresh, so a change can take a few minutes to appear.

Restore a purchase

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

On your device open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Catholic Widgets and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
