Privacy Policy

Catholic Widgets, by Tres Velas. Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Catholic Widgets shows the daily Gospel in the app and in widgets. It works without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos, and the app contains no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, theme, widget choices) and the daily readings are stored on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To know whether Pro features are unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (product, date, renewal status) together with a random identifier created by the app. RevenueCat also receives the identifier Apple assigns to your device for apps from the same developer (the vendor identifier), your device model, iOS version, app version, language settings, App Store country, the language chosen in the app and which version of a screen you were shown when we are comparing two versions. As with any internet service, RevenueCat's servers receive your IP address when the app contacts them. None of this is linked to your name, email address or Apple Account. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Usage statistics

The app sends usage events to PostHog, such as the app being opened, onboarding steps completed, the subscription screen being shown, a purchase being started or completed, and a widget being added. PostHog also tells the app which version of a screen to show when we are comparing two versions. These events carry a random identifier created on your device, along with your device type and model, iOS version, app version, screen size, language and region setting, time zone, the language chosen in the app, and whether the device is on Wi-Fi or a cellular connection. As with any internet service, PostHog's servers receive your IP address when events are sent. This data is not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. PostHog stores this data in the United States. See the PostHog privacy policy.

Content downloads

The app downloads the daily readings file from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host receives your IP address when the file is requested. See the GitHub privacy statement.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings. To ask about or request deletion of purchase data held by RevenueCat or usage data held by PostHog, write to us at the address below. Because this data is stored under random identifiers and not under your name or email address, we may need your help to find which records are yours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
